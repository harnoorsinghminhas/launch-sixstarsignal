What we know, and what’s rumor

Every line names its source, and every line carries a label. Confirmed means the company said it. Reported means a news outlet reports it. Not announced means there is no official word.

What we know

  • Confirmed

    Launch date: November 19, 2026.

    Sources: Rockstar Games; Take-Two earnings release, August 7, 2026

  • Confirmed

    Platforms: PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

    Source: Take-Two announcement, June 24, 2026

  • Confirmed

    Announced prices: $79.99 Standard Edition, $99.99 Ultimate Edition.

    Source: Take-Two announcement, June 24, 2026

  • Confirmed

    Pre-orders opened June 25, 2026. Pre-orders and purchases before November 20, 2026 include the Vintage Vice City Pack.

    Sources: Rockstar Newswire; Take-Two announcement

  • Confirmed

    Official videos: Trailer 1, Trailer 2 and “An Extended Look.” Watch them on Rockstar’s own page.

    Source: Rockstar Games

  • Reported

    Over 170 animal species, plus localized storms and tropical hurricanes, in the game’s Florida-inspired setting.

    Source: Game Informer cover story, September 29, 2026: animals, weather

What’s rumor, or not announced

  • Not announced

    A PC version. Rockstar’s official platform list is PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Any PC date you see is a guess.

    Source: Rockstar Games platform list, checked October 3, 2026

  • Rumor

    Another delay. Take-Two’s August 7 earnings release still refers to the November 19 launch. A new delay claim is a rumor until Rockstar or Take-Two says so.

    Source: Take-Two earnings release

  • Rumor

    Anything from a screenshot, a “my cousin works there” post or a forum thread. We label it Rumor and keep it out of the headlines until a company source backs it up.

How we label: Confirmed needs Rockstar, Take-Two or a filing. Reported needs a named news outlet. Everything else is Rumor.
